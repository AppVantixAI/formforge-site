Product capabilities

What makes specification lock possible.

Modules sized by the job — each with what it does and why it helps you get designed in first.

Capability

Parametric CAD logic

We rebuild your frozen STEP as a recipe: named variables and relationships — not a fixed photograph of one size.

Why it matters: Parameterization is the product. One model becomes thousands of valid parts; the website just turns the knobs.

Capability

Web configurator UI

Buyers and sales pick approved options in a branded browser experience — mid-call, on a share link.

Why it matters: Each slider is wired to a model variable. The site doesn’t know CAD; it sends numbers to the knobs we exposed.

Capability

3D viewer

Live 3D preview as options change. Rotate, inspect, and confirm before anyone exports.

Why it matters: No ambiguity about what they asked for — they see it before the quote goes out.

Capability

Rule / validation engine

Only approved combinations ship. Constraints cut invalid configurations before they hit engineering.

Why it matters: Trustworthy STEP — designed-in only happens if the model is correct.

Capability

STEP / export pipeline

When they’re happy, the backend rebuilds their exact config and exports STEP — the universal CAD handshake.

Why it matters: They need geometry for their assembly, not a picture. STEP works in SolidWorks, Inventor, Creo, NX — anyone can use it.

Capability

Admin & publishing

Publish families, manage share links, and keep catalog state under ops control — not a shared drive.

Why it matters: You own the configurator that locks spec — not a one-off email attachment.
