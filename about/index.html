Mission

Get designed in first. Keep the buyer designing.

Too many manufacturers still run configure-to-order as a dimension PDF into five inboxes. Generic ecommerce cannot express the rules. Waiting on a redraw loses the layout slot. VendorCAD is the CAD-backed configurator that makes your STEP the one they build around — without replacing your CAD seat or PLM.

For the business: Higher win rate. Comparison-shopping buyers design around the supplier who delivers a correct model instantly — before competitors respond.

For their customer: No more PDF guesswork or back-and-forth decoding dimensions. See it, get the STEP file, keep designing. Quote requests carry the exact spec.

Why now

Comparison shopping is the default. Speed and clarity win.

Mid-market manufacturers lose deals not because they are expensive, but because a competitor’s model landed in the assembly first. The category is ready for configurators serious enough for engineering and fast enough for the buyer’s layout clock — delivered as DFY implementation, not a toolkit dump.

Principles

How we build and sell.

  • 01

    Specification lock is the job

    The supplier who puts a correct STEP in the buyer’s assembly first usually wins. We build for that race — not for prettier 3D alone.

  • 02

    Ambiguity is the enemy

    PDF dimension forms and “interpreted to the best of our ability” quotes bake in doubt. The model must be the request.

  • 03

    Always both halves

    Business buyers pay for win rate. Their customers need see-it / get-the-file / keep-designing. Say both — never only drafting-cost savings.

  • 04

    No fake proof

    We do not invent customer logos, ROI percentages, or features that are still placeholders. Credibility compounds.

Credibility

Sales-led, implementation-led.

VendorCAD ships as B2B SaaS plus done-for-you catalog work. Commercial terms stay honest: discovery, pilot, then catalog. Case studies publish as pilots approve — we will not invent logos or win-rate percentages. Product app surfaces (catalog, configure, share, admin) stay industrial and usable, not marketing chrome bolted onto the studio.
