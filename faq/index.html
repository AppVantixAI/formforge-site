When a buyer needs a custom industrial part, they often RFQ several suppliers. Whoever gets a correct 3D model into their assembly first usually gets designed in — and usually wins the order. VendorCAD makes that supplier you: unambiguous geometry, instant STEP, exact-spec quote.

A STEP of a finished part is a perfect record of what you build — geometry, shop conventions, real detail. It is frozen: no knobs, no adjustable length. We use it as the reference (the spec), then remodel it as a parametric recipe so dimensions become variables. You cannot make a photograph adjustable by editing the photograph.
